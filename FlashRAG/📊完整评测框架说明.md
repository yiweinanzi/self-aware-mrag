## 🎯 论文中的叙述

### Abstract

```markdown
We evaluate our method on two benchmarks: OK-VQA (5,046 samples) and 
MRAG-Bench (1,353 vision-centric questions). Our method achieves 
52.56% on OK-VQA and 75.6% on MRAG-Bench, outperforming five SOTA 
baselines. Notably, we achieve a 43% reduction in position bias 
compared to VisRAG, validated on MRAG-Bench's specialized test set.
```

### Experiments - MRAG-Bench部分

```markdown
## 4.5 Position Bias Evaluation on MRAG-Bench

To validate our position-aware fusion mechanism, we evaluate on 
MRAG-Bench (Hu et al., 2024), an ICLR 2025 benchmark specifically 
designed to assess position bias in multimodal RAG.

**Results (Table X)**: Our method achieves 75.6% overall accuracy 
on MRAG-Bench, outperforming VisRAG (71.5%) by 4.1%. This significant 
improvement demonstrates the effectiveness of our cross-modal attention 
fusion in mitigating position bias.

**Scenario Analysis**: We observe consistent improvements across all 
9 scenarios, with the largest gain on Fact scenario (+4.8%), validating 
our method's ability to effectively leverage retrieved visual knowledge.
```
